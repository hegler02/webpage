# Card interaction contract

- A navigation card has one destination and one native anchor spanning its image, title, copy and whitespace.
- Do not add a duplicate CTA, arrow or nested link inside a navigation card.
- Cards without a verified destination remain static; no hover lift or pointer cursor.
- Shared interaction tokens live in pages/profile/styles.css: card-action-duration, card-action-ease, card-action-lift, card-action-border, card-focus-width and card-focus-inset.
- Preserve visible keyboard focus, touch scrolling, and reduced-motion preferences.
- Playback, filters, carousel navigation and actions with multiple destinations retain distinct controls.
- Home/archive cards are generated by tools/render_archive.py. Work introduction cards are generated by tools/render_hwasan_intro.py and tools/render_birthday_intro.py. Change generators, not just their output.

Validation: run tools/release_gate.py and verify home, archive, work and books at mobile width. Check full-card native links, no nested controls, no horizontal page overflow and keyboard focus.
